Three boundaries worth remembering

Explore the live simulator
01 / DETECTION

The sensor makes the decision.

Crossings are detected once, in a standalone service. Closing a browser does not stop monitoring. The current data source is simulated.

02 / AUTHORITY

The relay owns shared state.

Alerts, acknowledgements, settings, users, and sessions live in SQLite. The relay checks identity and role before routing messages.

03 / PRESENTATION

Browsers project that state.

The monitor combines a fast reading buffer with Redux. The control room uses a reducer for its alarm list and acknowledgement actions.
